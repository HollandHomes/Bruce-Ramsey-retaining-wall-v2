Project Overview

Installation of erosion-control retaining walls behind the main rear concrete slab to prevent hillside runoff and soil from washing onto the pad. Below are two layout options—Layout 1: Two Separate Retaining Walls matching the end of the stairs (~46' total length @ 22" height) and Layout 2: One Unified Retaining Wall extending beyond the stairs (~65' total length @ 36.75" average height + buried bottom base course)—paired with your choice of Concrete Retaining Wall Blocks or Treated Railroad Ties.

Active Scope: Layout 2 — One Unified Retaining Wall extending beyond the stairs (~65' total length @ 36.75" average visible height + 1 extra buried bottom base layer) backfilled with clean washed drainage gravel and lined with landscape filter fabric.

1. Select Wall Layout & Dimensions

Step 1

Choose between two separate retaining walls ending at the stairs or one continuous unified wall extending past the stairs into the hillside slope.

Layout 1: Two Separate Walls (To Stairs)
~46' Total Length • 22" Finished Height

  • Single uninterrupted wall run extending beyond the stairs
  • Extra hillside excavation for more room & buried bottom layer
  • ~8.5 cubic yards of clean washed drainage gravel backfill

Appendix: Schedule, Terms & Conditions

Estimated Project Schedule

Days 1–2: Hillside Cutback, Trenching & Base Compaction

Cut back the hillside bank to give extra room behind the slab, dig the footing trench along the 65' run, pack down a level crushed stone base, and receive gravel & block delivery.

Days 3–4: Buried Bottom Course, Wall Stacking & Drainage

Set and level the buried bottom base row below ground, stack courses to ~36.75" average visible height, line the back bank with landscape filter fabric, lay perforated drain pipe, and backfill with ~8.5 cubic yards of clean washed drainage gravel.

Day 5 (Applicable to Concrete Block Option): Top Capstones & Final Cleanup

Cut end capstones to fit cleanly, glue down all top capstones with heavy-duty masonry adhesive, level top gravel grade, and complete final site cleanup.

Scope Clarifications

  • Buried Bottom Row: Both layouts include 1 full base layer buried below ground level to lock the wall firmly into the slope.
  • Drainage Protection: All backfill uses clean washed drainage gravel separated from the soil by landscape filter fabric along with a perforated drain pipe to keep water from building up behind the wall.
  • Layout Comparison: Layout 1 creates two separate walls ending at the stairs (~46' total @ 22" height); Layout 2 forms one unified continuous wall extending beyond the stairs (~65' total @ 36.75" average height + bottom base row).

Conditions & Exclusions

  • Underground Obstacles: Quote assumes standard soil digging. Large buried boulders, solid rock ledges, or unmarked utility lines requiring heavy rock-breaking equipment will be addressed via change order.
  • Material Pricing: Block, gravel, and timber pricing locked for 30 days from date of issue.
