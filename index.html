Appendix: Schedule, Terms & Conditions

Estimated Project Schedule

Days 1–2: Embankment Cutback, Trenching & Base Compaction

Cut back hillside slope to provide added slab clearance, excavate deep footing trench across 65 LF, install and plate-compact CR-6 base pad, and receive bulk aggregate & block delivery.

Days 3–4: Buried Base Course, Wall Stacking & Drainage

Set and level the buried base tier below grade, stack courses to ~36.75" average visible height, line cut bank with non-woven filter fabric, install 4" perforated drain pipe, and backfill with ~8.5 cu. yds. of #57 washed gravel.

Day 5 (Applicable to Concrete Block Option): Coping, Capstones & Final Grade

Cut mitered end caps, adhere universal coping stones with polyurethane masonry adhesive, dress top gravel grade, and complete final site cleanup.

Scope Clarifications

  • Buried Footing Course: Includes 1 full extra course set below finished grade (~42"–44" total structural wall height) to anchor against lateral earth pressure.
  • Slope Drainage: All backfill uses clean #57 angular gravel (~8.5 cu. yds.) lined with geotextile fabric and 70' perforated drain pipe to eliminate hydrostatic pressure behind the wall.
  • Expanded Clearance: Wall alignment is pushed back into the embankment to increase usable space behind the concrete slab.

Conditions & Exclusions

  • Hidden Subsurface Obstacles: Quote assumes standard soil excavation. Large buried boulders, solid rock shelves, or unmarked utility lines requiring heavy hydraulic breaking will be billed via change order.
  • Material Pricing: Block, aggregate, and timber pricing locked for 30 days from date of issue.
